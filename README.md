# Global AI Adoption & Business Impact Dashboard

An end-to-end Tableau dashboard that analyzes how enterprise AI adoption affects cost, productivity and revenue across industries and countries.

## Business Problem
How is Artificial Intelligence transforming business operations, efficiency and financial growth? This project measures the real ROI of AI integration by comparing adoption levels, costs, time saved and revenue before and after AI.

## Tools Used
- Tableau (dashboard design, calculated fields, KPI cards)
- Data cleaning and preparation

## Key Metrics (KPIs)
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Employees | 8.56M |
| AI Users | 5.13M |
| Avg. AI Adoption Rate | 65.04% |
| Hours Saved | 16.82M |
| Monthly AI Cost | 664.23M |
| Revenue Before AI | 10.87B |
| Revenue After AI | 11.97B |
| Total Revenue Difference | 1.11B |

## Dashboard Views
- **Cost vs Saving by Industry:** compares AI spend with savings across Technology, Telecom, Healthcare and Education
- **Pre vs Post-AI Revenue Analysis:** monthly revenue trend before and after AI
- **AI Adoption Rate by Industry:** Technology leads at 80.34%, Education is lowest at 51.79%
- **AI Adoption Trend (Nov 2025 - Sep 2026):** month-wise adoption movement
- **Country vs AI Users vs Monthly AI Cost:** country-level comparison

## Key Insights
- About 5.13M of 8.56M employees use AI, an average adoption rate of about 65%.
- AI automation saved over 16.82M hours.
- Revenue rose from 10.87B to 11.97B after AI adoption, a gain of about 1.11B.
- Technology and Banking have the highest adoption, while Education and Logistics lag behind.
